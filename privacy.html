Privacy Policy

Telegram ChatGPT Bridge is a private automation for the account owner's personal use.

Data access and use

Make connects to Google Drive and Google Sheets using permissions granted by the owner. OAuth permissions may allow access to a broader set of Drive files than the temporary folder actually used by this automation.

The automation transfers files, captions and links received through a Telegram bot. Google Sheets stores file names, timestamps, Telegram identifiers and processing status.

Storage and deletion

Temporary Google Drive copies are scheduled for deletion approximately 48 hours after receipt. Registry data remains in Google Sheets until deleted by the owner.

Third-party services

Data is processed through Telegram, Make, Google services and, when requested by the owner, ChatGPT, subject to the respective providers' privacy policies.

Control

The owner can delete stored data, disconnect the automation and revoke Google API permissions.

The website itself does not collect user data. No public access to the automation is offered.

Use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
