Telegram ChatGPT Bridge

This is a private, personal-use automation operated by a single account holder.

Files and links sent to a Telegram bot are transferred using Make to Google Drive and Google Sheets. They may subsequently be accessed through ChatGPT at the owner's request.

Temporary copies on Google Drive are scheduled for deletion after 48 hours. Processing records may remain in Google Sheets.

The service is not offered to the public.
